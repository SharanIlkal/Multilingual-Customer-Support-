8. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The multilingual customer-support intent classification system demonstrates strong classification performance across English, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, and Telugu. Among the evaluated approaches, the BiLSTM with Attention produced the strongest results, achieving 98.01% test accuracy, 97.93% Macro F1, and 98.02% Weighted F1. The evaluation also shows strong performance across all six languages, with language-specific accuracy ranging from 97.23% to 98.99%. The remaining errors are concentrated primarily in semantically related intent classes such as invoice retrieval/checking, order modification/tracking, cancellation/order changes, and refund-related intents.
